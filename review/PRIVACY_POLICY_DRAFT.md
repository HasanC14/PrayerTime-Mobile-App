## Location

PrayerTime uses location to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction. You can choose a city manually instead of granting device-location access. If you enable phone location, the app receives the approximate or precise location allowed by your phone settings. Automatic updates run while the app is in use; this version does not request background-location permission.

Coordinates are sent over HTTPS to LocationIQ to obtain a city name. City-search text is also sent to LocationIQ. Network requests necessarily expose connection information such as your IP address to the service. LocationIQ's processing is governed by its own applicable terms and privacy policy. Prayer-time and Qibla calculations run on the device. Once a location has been selected, the calculations work offline. Online search and city naming require a connection.

## Data stored on your device

PrayerTime stores your selected location, up to five recent locations, calculation preferences, appearance settings, and reminder preferences on the device. This version disables Android automatic application backup. Uninstalling the app removes its local application data; you can also use Android's app-storage controls to clear it. Contact the developer about questions concerning data handled by external services.

## Notifications and sensors

Notification permission is optional and is used for enabled local prayer reminders. Compass readings are used on the device to provide Qibla guidance. Notification delivery and sound depend on phone permissions, settings, and platform behavior.

## Accounts, analytics, and external links

This version does not offer user accounts and does not include an advertising or analytics SDK in its application source. Recheck this statement if the production build adds any such service.

The optional support link opens an external website, which may handle payment and personal information under its own policies. PrayerTime does not process card details in the app.
