# Quran reflection collection

The collection contains **54 Quran-based reflections** across prayer, remembrance, hope, repentance, patience, gratitude, family, honesty, justice, generosity, and everyday conduct. Each lesson includes an app-authored title, plain explanation, practical suggestion, contextual note, a short attributed English translation excerpt, and links to the complete passage and Tafsir Ibn Kathir (abridged) on Quran.com.

## Display and selection

The journal shows one explanation and practical suggestion, with an inline reference opening a scrollable context sheet. The sheet distinguishes the app's explanation from the translation excerpt. Full passages and named tafsir open externally; the card, excerpt, and contextual note work offline. All app-authored text supports English, Bangla, and Arabic. Quran excerpts remain in the attributed English translation and are labelled accordingly.

Weekly assignments and reading history are saved locally under `@PrayerTime:v2:reflectionHistory`. A week stays pinned across restarts and changes in language or journal entries, provided its content remains suitable. Returning to an old week shows its assigned lesson without consuming another one. Up to 156 weekly assignments and the current collection's seen IDs are retained. Assignments are made when the Journal screen is focused; they indicate the lesson offered, not proof that a person read it.

New weeks select an unused eligible lesson before repeating. Logging patterns favour a relevant theme, and a meaningful relative Fajr recording gap favours the preparation lesson if it is unread. Unread lessons outside the preferred theme remain available, avoiding repeats caused by a small theme pool. When the eligible pool is exhausted, that pool begins a new cycle and avoids an immediate repeat of the previous lesson. Some readers have fewer than 54 eligible lessons: sparse histories exclude warnings, and fully exempt weeks select lessons whose actions do not ask the reader to resume salah. If a pinned warning becomes unsuitable, an appropriate lesson replaces it.

A failed save retains the current session's assignments and shows a brief notice. An unreadable store is not overwritten. A later successful read merges any session-only assignments with the existing saved weeks; subsequent serialized session state preserves cycle rollovers even if writes fail. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes this local history.

The collection is assembled in `src/content/prayerReflections.ts`; the additional 42 lessons and their translations live in `src/content/additionalReflections.ts`. Translations for the original 12 are in `src/i18n/reflectionMessages.ts`. No journal data is uploaded and no interpretations are generated live. The personal feel comes from relevant selection and remembering prior assignments; the interface does not claim to be AI-generated.

## Appearance

New and missing or invalid appearance settings default to light. Explicitly saved light, dark, classic dark, and system choices are preserved. Widgets receive that same app appearance setting.

## Editorial status

Translation excerpts and contextual claims were checked against the linked Quran.com passages on 2026-11-04 and 2026-11-05; named tafsir links support further reading. Excerpts are from Dr. Mustafa Khattab's *The Clear Quran*. These checks are not independent scholarly approval. Qualified Islamic editorial review of the explanations and their Bangla/Arabic translations remains pending before public release. Do not label the collection scholar-reviewed without that review.
