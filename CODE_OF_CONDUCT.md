# Community code of conduct

PrayerTime welcomes contributors of different backgrounds, identities, and levels of experience. Treat contributors and users with respect.

- Give constructive feedback about the work and remain open to correction.
- Respect privacy and use sample data when sharing examples.
- Avoid harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual content directed at participants, and personal attacks.
- Keep religious discussions relevant to the app and respectful of differences in practice.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive threads, or limit participation. Enforcement should be proportionate to the behavior and consider its impact.

Report conduct concerns to the maintainer through [hasanbuilds.com](https://hasanbuilds.com), requesting a private channel when needed. Maintainers should protect reporters' privacy and handle reports fairly.
